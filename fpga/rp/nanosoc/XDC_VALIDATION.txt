--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Why no <rm>_rm.xdc for this RM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
rm_nanosoc is effectively SINGLE-CLOCK at DFX implementation:
  - the whole nanosoc core + the uart_axis_shim run on dut_clk (the shim's
    baud divider is a synchronous dut_clk counter; its 2-FF "sync" samples an
    internal dut_clk-domain loopback — single domain, no CDC, no exception);
  - the only other domain is the SW-DP on swd_clk, which is a firmware
    bit-banged CSR pin with NO create_clock in the static (partition-pins.md
    SWD group / shell-regmap.md SWDBB), so it carries no clock at link and the
    swd async group is an OOC-only convenience.
There is therefore nothing RM-internal to reapply scoped to the RP cell at
link, so rm_nanosoc ships nanosoc_ooc.xdc ONLY (no eth_ss-style _rm.xdc).
