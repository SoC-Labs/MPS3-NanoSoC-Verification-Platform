--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES / what the RM's internal timing surfaced
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HOLD (41 endpoints, WHS -0.178 ns) is on the phy_rmii_ref_clk <-> mii_tx/rx
   /2 crossing — the rmii_to_mii toggle-register-as-clock (mtx_clk/mrx_clk are
   plain FFs used as clocks, 0 BUFG). At OOC POST-SYNTH there is no placement or
   routing, so hold slack is not yet meaningful and is resolved by place/route;
   it is NOT an OOC sign-off blocker. It IS a real, previously-invisible design
   characteristic (a divided clock generated in fabric) that this contract now
   makes visible for the first time — worth watching at the full DFX
   implementation (where eth_ss_rm.xdc reapplies the same generated clocks +
   async group scoped to the RP cell).
2. dut_clk<->rtc_clk (PTP) and dut_clk<->DMA-SRAM are SINGLE-DOMAIN in this
   wrapper (rtc_clk = dut_clk, SRAM HCLK = dut_clk), so they needed NO async
   exception and time cleanly inside the dut_clk group (3953 endpoints). The
   partition-timing.md task listed them as candidate CDCs; they collapse to one
   domain in rp_eth_ss_wrapper's clock choices.
